# Components Overview

The sidebar is your central navigation hub. Here is a breakdown of what each module does:

### 📊 Dashboard
Your command center. It features your primary balance, quick-action logging buttons, progressive spending insights, interactive daily/monthly charts, and a carousel of your frequently used accounts. 

### 💳 Accounts
The portfolio manager. Segment your money into Banks, E-Wallets, On-hand Cash, and Investments. Transferring money between these accounts acts as an isolated internal movement and does not affect your overall Net Worth or Expense charts.

### 📓 Ledgers
The debt tracker. Create dedicated profiles for friends, family, or businesses. Log when you lend money or borrow funds, record partial repayments, and generate a printable PDF **Statement of Account** to settle debts professionally.

### 📈 Analytics
Deep-dive financial reporting. View dynamic cash-flow Sankey diagrams, progressive income vs. expense bar charts, and long-term trajectory graphs. Use the custom date selectors to isolate specific financial periods.

### 📋 Activity
Your raw transaction ledger. A complete, searchable history of every income, expense, and transfer. Features an advanced filter panel to isolate transactions by date, amount, category, or trip status.

### ✈️ Trips (Travel Mode)
A sandbox for event budgeting. Vacations ruin standard budget algorithms. By creating a Trip, your spending is isolated from your daily analytics. You can fund trips via dedicated savings goals and receive a "Spotify Wrapped"-style insight presentation upon completion.

### 🎯 Goals & Subscriptions
*   **Goals:** Track sinking funds for large purchases. Allocate a percentage of your monthly savings to automatically fund these goals over time.
*   **Subscriptions:** Track recurring payments (Netflix, AWS, Gym). Enable "Auto-log" to have the app automatically generate a transaction when the billing date arrives.

### 🚀 Savings Planner (Yield Optimizer)
An interactive catalog of digital banks. Input an amount to see exactly how to distribute your funds across tiered interest rates to maximize your passive income, complete with multi-year compound projections.

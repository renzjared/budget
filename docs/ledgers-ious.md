# Ledgers & IOUs

The Ledgers module is a specialized accounting tool designed to track debts, loans, and shared expenses with friends, family, or clients. It treats people as isolated "accounts" so you always know exactly who owes whom.

## 1. Creating a Ledger
1. Navigate to the **Ledgers** tab and click **+ New Ledger**.
2. Enter the name of the person or entity (e.g., "Roommates", "John Doe").
3. Set the currency you typically use to transact with them.

## 2. Logging Items (The Debt)
When you cover a bill or borrow money, you log an Item.
1. Click on the person's Ledger card and select **+ Add Item**.
2. Choose the direction: **I Lent Them** (they owe you) or **They Lent Me** (you owe them).
3. Enter the description and the cost.
4. **Funding Source (Optional):** If you paid for their lunch using your GCash account, you can select GCash here. The app will automatically deduct the money from your GCash balance while simultaneously recording the debt in the Ledger.

## 3. Logging Payments (The Settlement)
When someone pays you back (or you pay them back), you log a Payment.
1. Click **+ Log Payment** inside their Ledger.
2. Choose the direction: **They Paid Me** or **I Paid Them**.
3. **Crucial Step - Select Account:** You must select where the money went (e.g., your Bank account or On-hand Cash). 
4. *How it works:* The app will automatically reduce their outstanding debt in the Ledger, and instantly add the cash to your selected real-world account balance.

## 4. Generating Statements of Account
If a ledger gets complicated with dozens of borrowed items and partial repayments, you can generate a professional receipt to settle the math.
1. Open the Ledger details.
2. Click the **Document/Print icon** in the top right corner.
3. The app will generate a clean, itemized **Statement of Account** showing a complete history of lent items, applied payments, and the final outstanding balance.
4. Click **Save as PDF / Print** to export the statement and send it to the person.

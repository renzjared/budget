# Travel Mode ✈️

Vacations, out-of-town trips, and major life events can completely ruin your standard budgeting algorithms by injecting massive, unusual expenses into your daily averages. **Travel Mode** solves this by putting those expenses into a temporary quarantine.

## 1. Starting a Trip
1. Click the **Manage Trip** button on the Dashboard, or navigate to the **Trips** tab and click **+ Start a Trip**.
2. **Name your Trip:** (e.g., "Japan 2026").
3. **Set the Budget Source:** 
   * *Saved Goal:* Link the trip directly to a Savings Goal you've been funding. As you spend on the trip, money is deducted from that specific goal instead of your daily cash flow.
   * *Fixed Amount:* Set a hard, standalone allowance.
   * *Incoming Funds:* Fund the trip purely based on money you log as "Income" while the trip is active.
4. **Customize Categories:** Set percentage allowances for trip-specific categories like ACCOMMODATION, FOOD, and TRANSPORT.

## 2. Logging Trip Expenses
While a trip is active, the app enters **Travel Theme** (the UI accents turn purple).
* When you add an Expense or Income, a new toggle appears: **Log to an active Trip**.
* Check this box to assign the transaction to your vacation.
* These transactions will *not* affect your standard Monthly/Weekly budget trackers on the Dashboard.

## 3. Trip Dashboard & Insights
Navigate to the **Trips** tab while traveling to see your specialized command center.
* It displays your Total Trip Budget, Spent Today, and Remaining Balance.
* It tracks progress bars for your specific travel categories (e.g., tracking exactly how much of your budget you've blown on FOOD).
* **Ending the Trip:** Click **End Trip** when you return home to restore the app to its standard budgeting behavior.

## 4. Spotify-Wrapped Summaries
Once a trip is completed, it moves to your Past Trips history.
* Click **✨ View Insights** on any past trip.
* The app will generate a highly visual, animated, "Spotify Wrapped"-style presentation of your vacation.
* It highlights your most frequent merchants, biggest single flex (most expensive purchase), and timeline data.
* You can click **Share Image** to instantly export a beautiful graphic of your financial travels to share with friends.

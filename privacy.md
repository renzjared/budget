# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** June 2026

Your privacy is critically important. This policy outlines how your data is handled within the Budget Tracker.

### 1. Data We Collect
*   **Authentication Data:** When you log in with Discord, we receive basic profile information (Discord ID, Avatar).
*   **Financial Data:** Transactions, custom categories, notes, account balances, and budget configurations that you actively input or sync via CSV.
*   **Settings:** UI preferences such as Dark Theme toggles and currency symbols.

### 2. How We Use Your Data
Your data is used strictly to provide the core functionality of the Budget Tracker. We use Supabase (a secure backend-as-a-service) to safely store and retrieve your ledger across devices. 

### 3. Data Ownership & Security
*   **You own your data.** All data is linked directly to your unique ID.
*   **Row Level Security (RLS)** is enforced at the database level, meaning no other user can query, read, or modify your financial records.
*   We **do not** sell, share, or distribute your financial data to third-party advertisers.

### 4. Third-Party Services
*   **Supabase:** Provides our database and authentication infrastructure.
*   **Discord:** Provides the secure OAuth login framework.

### 5. Data Deletion
If you wish to purge your data or delete your account, please reach out via the Contact Us page.

# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Renz Jared Rolle ("we," "our," or "the developer") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Budget Tracker ("the Service"), as part of the Renz-bot Project.

### 1. Information We Collect
We collect the minimum amount of information required to provide you with a functional and secure financial tracking experience.
*   **Authentication Data:** We utilize Discord OAuth to manage user accounts. We receive your unique Discord ID, your username, and your public avatar URL.
*   **Financial & Transactional Data:** This includes all data you voluntarily input: transaction descriptions, amounts, categories, merchant names, notes, and account balances.
*   **Application Settings:** Preferences such as theme (light/dark mode), currency selections, budget cycles, and privacy mode toggles.
*   **System Metadata:** Technical metadata necessary for maintaining application state, including trip configurations and goal allocations.

### 2. Use of Information
Your data is used exclusively to facilitate the core operations of the Service:
*   **Data Synchronization:** Storing your ledger so it remains available across multiple devices.
*   **Budgetary Analytics:** Calculating your net worth, cash flow trends, and budget progress.
*   **Application Stability:** Logged technical data is used solely for troubleshooting and performance optimization.

### 3. Data Security and Ownership
*   **Ownership:** You retain full ownership of all data you input.
*   **Security Measures:** All data is stored via Supabase, which enforces Row Level Security (RLS) at the database level. This ensures that your financial records are isolated and accessible only to you.
*   **No Third-Party Sharing:** We do not sell, rent, or distribute your financial information to third-party advertisers, data aggregators, or unauthorized third parties.

### 4. Third-Party Integrations
*   **Supabase:** Serves as our primary backend infrastructure for database hosting and authentication.
*   **Discord:** Provides the secure framework for user account management and authentication.

### 5. Cookies and Local Storage
The Service uses local browser storage (LocalStorage) to cache exchange rates, UI settings, and temporary session data to improve load times. You can clear this data at any time via your browser settings or the "Clear All Data" function within the Service.

### 6. Data Deletion
*   **User-Initiated Deletion:** You have the right to purge all stored data at any time via the "Clear All Data" feature found in the Settings menu.
*   **Permanence:** Please note that all data deletion actions are permanent and irreversible.
